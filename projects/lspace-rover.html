Overview

From January to June 2024 I was part of NASA's L'Space Mission Concept Academy, run with the ASU Mars Space Flight Facility. Our team developed N.O.M.A.D, a Mars rover mission concept, through a full design review cycle.

What I did

  • Modeled the rover in CATIA V5: chassis, rocker-bogie legs and wheels, instrument mast and arm, science instruments, solar panel and antenna.
  • Drew the 5-sheet rover engineering drawing package in Siemens NX: isometric, front, side and top views plus the parts list.
  • Contributed to the Mission Design Review, including the parts manufacturing section.
