Separation Lock Test Rig Redesign

Re-designing the vehicle and hold-down clamp separation lock test rig at Rocket Factory Augsburg, then running the campaign as Test Conductor.

Organization
Rocket Factory Augsburg
Role
Test Engineer Intern / Test Conductor
Timeline
May 2026 to Aug 2026
Tools
Pneumatics, DAQ, 3D CAD, P&ID

Overview

Separation locks are what hold the stages of a launch vehicle together, and the hold-down clamps keep it on the pad, until the moment they have to let go. During my 2026 Test Engineering internship at Rocket Factory Augsburg, I re-designed the test rig for the vehicle and hold-down clamp separation locks, covering the pneumatic system, structure and data acquisition.

Impact

Old rigRedesigned rig
  • Set-up time down by about 70%, helium usage down by about 80% and rig weight down by about 50%, while improving reliability and data quality.
  • Planned a test campaign that proved the root cause of an anomalous increase in opening pressures.
  • Led, as Test Conductor, actuation, fatigue, ultimate load, environmental, tensile/compression and cycle life tests.
  • Analyzed and characterized geometric degradation and opening pressure across conditions, and handled routine rig maintenance and refurbishment of tested items.

The rest of the internship

Overall I designed (P&ID and 3D CAD), manufactured and operated 7 test rigs for components like separation locks, hold-down clamps, COPVs, cryogenic valve actuation and cable/pressurization tunnels. I also led pad operations for burst and hydrostatic tests on valves, COPVs and tanks, and ran cryogenic, vacuum, vibration and thermal (Thermotron) testing.
