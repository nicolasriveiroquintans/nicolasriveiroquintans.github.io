01 / About

Propulsion, test & the numbers behind them

I'm Nico, and I'm finishing a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University in Daytona Beach (graduating December 2027, 4.0 GPA, Dean's List every semester).

Most of my time goes into rocket propulsion. At ERPL I went from assembling a cryogenic feed system to operating it during hotfires, and I've built tools for the team like a COPV pressurization model and a flame diverter heat-transfer simulator. In industry I've worked on a Flight Termination System and on test rigs at Rocket Factory Augsburg, and on Rolls-Royce and Pratt & Whitney engine repairs at Delta TechOps.

I care about tests that are safe, repeatable and actually answer the question you ran them for.
